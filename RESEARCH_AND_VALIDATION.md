# Research and Validation Notes

Research date: 20 September 2026

## Scope and honesty statement

This is directional desk research used to form and test visual hypotheses for a HeartStamp application portfolio. It is not presented as commissioned consumer research, direct access to HeartStamp's internal library, or statistically valid Gen Z user testing.

## Evidence matrix

| Research lens | Observed signal | Portfolio translation | What was deliberately avoided |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adobe 2026 creative forecast | Sensory texture, emotional connection, playful surrealism, locally grounded humanity | Paper grain, watercolor bleed, visible repair, emotional gestures and surreal-but-readable metaphors | Sterile AI polish and surrealism without emotional purpose |
| Pinterest Predicts 2026 | Curated individuality, poetcore, cool blue, glitchy glamour, celestial/alien cues and refined maximalism | Cosmic Scrapbook, edge glitches, tactile ephemera, layered composition and controlled maximalism | Copying a named micro-trend as a complete style |
| Etsy marketplace language | Gen Z cards are repeatedly framed as funny, sassy, irreverent and meme-aware | Relationship-specific titles such as "Same Sky, Different Wi-Fi" and "Proud of You, Somehow" | Disposable slang, copied jokes and short-lived meme references |
| Greeting-card category reporting | Younger consumers value authenticity and cards that address heavier or less traditional life moments | Apology, difficult-day support, breakup recovery, missed milestones and chosen family | Limiting the library to birthdays and conventional sentiment |
| Hallmark category evidence | Cards create a stronger feeling of being personally noticed than email or text | Emotion-first concepts in which one gesture carries the message | Treating the card as decoration rather than relationship media |

## Core audience hypothesis

For this work, "Gen Z native" means emotionally direct, visually tactile, culturally current and self-aware. It does not automatically mean louder colors, slang, meme templates or visual chaos.

## Original style hypotheses

### Glitch Garden Letters

- Research signals: tactile feeling, refined maximalism, glitchy glamour and playful surrealism.
- Stable DNA: watercolor blooms, fluorescent halftone, edge-only glitches, one oversized focal form, minimum 30% message-safe space.
- Tested occasions: chaotic birthday, pride/admiration and surviving a difficult week.

### Tender Brutalism

- Research signals: emotional authenticity, visible imperfection and heavier life moments.
- Stable DNA: sheltering brutalist geometry, vulnerable paper characters, restrained emotion and one repaired or imperfect object.
- Tested occasions: apology, difficult-day support and complicated gratitude.

### Cosmic Scrapbook

- Research signals: celestial/alien imagery, personal ephemera, nostalgia and chosen relationships.
- Stable DNA: separate worlds connected by one luminous thread, torn-paper depth, personal objects and unreadable interface fragments.
- Tested occasions: long-distance friendship, chosen-family holiday and a missed milestone.

## Controlled variation result

Six additional images were generated using fixed style rules with variable occasions and emotions. All six retained their intended palette families, material logic and usable message space while producing distinct emotional scenarios. This is a small portfolio test, not proof of industrial-scale consistency; a production validation would run a larger sample and record failure rates.

## Proposed Gen Z Validation Plan

Not conducted. Recruit 8–12 consenting US-based adults aged 18–29. Randomize unlabelled systems and variations. Ask for unaided emotion recognition and recipient fit before 1–5 emotional clarity, relevance and willingness-to-send ratings. Record open responses and disagreements. Proposed iteration triggers: less than 70% intended-emotion recognition or median relevance below 4/5. These are working thresholds, not validated benchmarks. Revise one rule group and retest. Publish anonymized findings, sample limitations and prompt versions; avoid unnecessary personal data.
